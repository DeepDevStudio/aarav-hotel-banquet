FAQs

Questions, answered

When do bookings open?

Bookings open from 1st January. You can call or WhatsApp us now to reserve your date and book a site visit.

Where is Aarav Hotel & Banquet?

Plot No. E-12, Rameshwar Park, near Jagmohan Hospital, on the main road in Loni, Ghaziabad.

How many guests can the hall hold?

The AC banquet hall is set up for functions from about 100 guests to grand weddings of 500+ guests.

Do you provide catering and décor?

Yes. Packages include in-house catering, stage and décor. You can also customise the menu and theme.

Are rooms available for the wedding family?

Yes, comfortable AC rooms are available at the venue for the couple, family and guests.
