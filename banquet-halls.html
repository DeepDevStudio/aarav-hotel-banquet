FAQs

About the venue

Is the hall fully air-conditioned?

Yes, the banquet hall, dining area and rooms are fully air-conditioned.

Can we bring our own decorator or caterer?

Please talk to our team. We prefer in-house catering and décor for quality, but we can discuss your needs.

Is there power backup?

Yes, full power backup keeps lights, sound and AC running throughout your event.

Can we visit the hall before booking?

Of course. Call or WhatsApp us to book a site visit at a time that suits you.
