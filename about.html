Our Story

A grand address, close to home

Aarav Hotel & Banquet was created with one simple wish: to give the families of Loni and Ghaziabad a beautiful, comfortable and truly grand place to celebrate, without travelling far.

Every detail, from the elegant hall and lighting to the stage, the food and the rooms, is planned so you can enjoy your function stress-free while your guests feel welcomed like family. Whether it is a big wedding or an intimate get-together, we treat every event as our own.
